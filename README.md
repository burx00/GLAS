GLAS – Your Voice, Your Safety

GLAS is an iOS safety application designed to create safer and more connected communities through technology and collective awareness.

Features
- Interactive safety map with reported incidents
- SOS button for quick access to emergency assistance
- Incident reporting and history
- Directions to the nearest police station
- Community safety awareness

Technologies
- Swift & SwiftUI
- Apple MapKit
- CoreLocation
- Xcode

Project Status

Currently in prototype development. Incident data is for demonstration purposes. Direct emergency-service integration and secure anonymous reporting are planned features.

Our Mission

To empower individuals, encourage community participation, and make public spaces safer for everyone.
